# Service centre contact and opening hours

The fictional municipal service centre is open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 15:00.

Residents may contact the service centre by phone during opening hours or by using the general contact form.

The service centre can help route questions to the correct municipal unit, but it does not make individual case decisions.
