# General appeal guidance

Residents who receive a written decision may usually find information about appeal routes in the decision letter.

The municipality may provide general guidance about where appeal information is normally found, but automated tools must not decide whether an appeal will succeed.

Questions about deadlines, individual rights, exceptions or case-specific appeal strategy must be reviewed by a human caseworker.
