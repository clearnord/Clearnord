# Evidence Requirements

The scorecard distinguishes between an answer and evidence for that answer. Vendor statements can be useful for early dialogue, but public-sector pilot readiness normally requires documentary evidence that can be reviewed, archived and attached to procurement or governance records.

## Evidence Types

- Policy document: published or contractually provided policy text.
- Technical document: architecture, logging, access control, retention or export documentation.
- Contract draft: draft clause, data processing agreement, service terms or exit provision.
- Security pack: current security whitepaper, certification summary, penetration-test summary or equivalent controlled evidence.
- Test record: language, accessibility, terminology or model-change test report.
- Vendor statement: written statement from the vendor without stronger supporting documentation.

## Weak Evidence Signals

- evidence is only a vendor statement
- evidence is incomplete
- evidence is not current
- evidence does not cover the specific public-sector workflow
- evidence cannot be exported for audit or contract records

## Human Review

Human procurement and governance review remains necessary where evidence is partial, ambiguous, rights-affecting, security-relevant or dependent on contract wording.
