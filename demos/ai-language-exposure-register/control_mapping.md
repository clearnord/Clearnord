# Control Mapping

This demo uses transparent control IDs to make AI and language exposure review easy to discuss before a pilot, procurement or broader implementation.

The controls are derived from the ClearNord public-sector AI and language governance framework, especially the principles of human accountability, source-grounded operation, controlled multilingual quality, risk-based use, auditability and vendor readiness.

| Control ID | Control | Demo expectation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GOV-01 | Accountable ownership | A named municipal role owns the workflow and its governance record. |
| GOV-02 | Defined purpose | The workflow has a clear purpose, scope and intended use. |
| GOV-03 | Lifecycle review | The system has a review frequency, last review date and next review date. |
| DAT-01 | Data classification | The record identifies data categories and whether personal, special-category or confidential information is involved. |
| DAT-02 | Data minimisation | The workflow limits input data to what is necessary for the stated purpose. |
| DAT-03 | Approved processing route | Processing location, tool route and use constraints are documented before use. |
| HUM-01 | Human review requirement | A human review step is required before material use, publication or escalation. |
| HUM-02 | Authorised approval | A role is authorised to approve, reject or restrict use. |
| HUM-03 | Escalation and stop authority | The workflow defines when uncertainty, risk or missing safeguards must stop use. |
| LNG-01 | Language and audience requirements | Language, audience and accessibility expectations are documented. |
| LNG-02 | Terminology and quality control | Terminology, meaning preservation and language-quality checks are required where relevant. |
| VEN-01 | Vendor transparency | Provider type, tool route and vendor-related dependency are documented. |
| VEN-02 | Processing-location evidence | Hosting and processing-location evidence is documented before broader use. |
| AUD-01 | Decision logging | Review, approval and lifecycle decisions can be logged and explained later. |
| AUD-02 | Source traceability | Outputs can be traced to approved source material where claims or public guidance are involved. |
| EVA-01 | Pre-pilot evaluation | A pre-pilot review confirms scope, risk, controls, limitations and stop conditions. |

These controls are demo governance controls. They are not a legal checklist, statutory classification scheme or compliance certification.
