# Risk Matrix

This demo uses internal governance labels to support early portfolio review. The labels are not statutory or legal classifications, and they are not final AI Act classifications.

## Governance Priority

| Priority | Meaning in this demo | Typical response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Limited internal or public-language support with no personal data, no rights-affecting impact and clear human review. | Use with documented conditions and routine lifecycle review. |
| Medium | Public-facing or operational language workflow, or limited personal-data exposure, where human review and source controls are required. | Approve only with conditions, monitoring and documented fallback. |
| High | Sensitive service context, confidential information, special-category data, procurement uncertainty or weak safeguards. | Restrict until stronger controls, review and evidence are in place. |
| Critical | Stop condition or unresolved exposure that could affect rights, vulnerable groups, sensitive data or public trust. | Do not approve for real use until the stop condition is resolved. |

## Approved-Use Status

| Status | Meaning in this demo |
| --- | --- |
| Approved | The workflow is permitted within the documented scope and controls. |
| Approved with conditions | The workflow may proceed only under listed limitations, mitigations and review requirements. |
| Restricted | The workflow is not generally approved and may only proceed in a narrow, controlled or synthetic-data setting. |
| Not approved | The workflow must not be piloted or used with real operational data until major gaps are resolved. |

The matrix is intended for municipal governance conversations before pilot, procurement or implementation. It does not replace legal review, DPIA work, procurement assessment, security approval or domain-owner approval.
